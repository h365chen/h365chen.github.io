Professional Summary

I recently earned a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo under the supervision of Dr. Paul A.S. Ward. My research focuses on computer science and engineering education, developing data-driven models to understand learning processes and designing high-quality automated assessments that provide effective formative feedback to support learning.
